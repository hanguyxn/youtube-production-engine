- Versioned model artifacts: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.0
- NumPy: https://numpy.org/doc/stable/license.html
- SoundFile/libsndfile: https://github.com/bastibe/python-soundfile

- imageio-ffmpeg and bundled FFmpeg: https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg
